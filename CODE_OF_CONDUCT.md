# Code of Conduct & Content Bar

This project is funny. It is not mean. The two are not in tension — the whole
craft here is keeping it professional *and* funny at the same time, in separate
layers: the lesson is real, the delivery is a laugh.

## The one rule for humor

**The joke is with people, never at them.** We laugh *with* the guy who force-
pushed to main. We laugh *with* the accent — it's an affectionate portrait, and
plenty of us have it. We do not laugh *at* an ethnic group, a race, a class, a
neighborhood's income, or a person's expense.

If you're the one running the bit, you go first in the pratfall. "Here's me
breaking my own repo" earns the right to show someone else's mess.

## Content that will not be merged

- Slurs or terms whose primary purpose is to demean an ethnic, racial, religious,
  or LGBTQ+ group.
- "Slang" that's just an insult aimed at a specific neighborhood's residents by
  income or background.
- Anything sexual involving minors, or that sexualizes a real named person.

## Offensive historical terms

A dialect includes ugly words people really said. So the translator can
*understand* input, maintainers may add such terms with `offensive: true` — but
they are stored for **reverse lookup only** and the skill will **never emit
them** in a translation. These are added by maintainers with care, not through
open PRs.

## People

Be kind to first-timers. Most contributors here have never opened a pull request
and are doing something genuinely uncomfortable. Snark at a nervous beginner gets
*you* moderated, not them. That's the deal.
